## Mathematical checks

The detailed proof records explain the following conclusions.

- Independent Poisson batch-type coordinates establish the covariance identities and the full product-space dependence structure. The countable extensions use the appropriate monotone or centered $L^2$ limits.
- The ordered-pair bracket representation includes the diagonal $a_{ii}=1/2$. Its load is at most $L_B=B(4B-3)/2$, and the hub construction approaches that value. Sharpness is claimed for this coefficient-load calculation, not for the best possible bracket-variance constant.
- The jump-moment powers, conditional smoothing lemma, stopped Gaussian completion, and deterministic-grid limits support the stated $1/5$ and logarithmic fourth-root normal-approximation bounds.
- The de-Poissonization expansion retains the diagonal terms and subtracts $n m'(n)^2$; its remainder is an absolute $o(1)$ for a fixed law. The square-root-window argument is qualitative and does not supply a universal fixed-sample rate depending only on variance.
- The unbounded-batch truncation controls both terms in the second moment of the deleted compound-Poisson mass. The counterexample has variance divergence at all sufficiently large times but a non-Gaussian subsequential limit; loss of uniform integrability explains its limiting second moment.
- The actual-variance MGF calculation has the stated denominator, and the moderate-deviation proof controls the exceptional bracket event under the exponential tilt.
- Threshold proofs use entire incident Poisson paths. The growing-threshold scale is $rB^2/V_r$; the moderate-deviation extension is stated for fixed $r$.
- Weighted discovery uses nonnegative weights and load bound $2W^2$. The signed-weight variance inequality alone does not imply a variance-normalized CLT for arbitrary signed weights.
